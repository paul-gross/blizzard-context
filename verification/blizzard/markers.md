# The markers a matrix row carries (`bzh:matrix-markers`)

The **Gap** annotation the [matrix](../blizzard.md) rows and the spokes beside this one carry — what it names, and when
to write one.

## Gap markers

A row that precedes its proof states the intended command and carries **Gap (phase N)**; drop the marker in the change
that lands and proves the method. A command's existence alone does not establish its stated proof.

One Gap stands against the inventory rather than any single row: the tag `release` workflow's full-suite tiers reuse the
push-verified multi-repo setup but have not yet been exercised under a real `v*` tag.
